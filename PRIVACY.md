# AspeQt — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 July 2026_

AspeQt is an open-source emulator of Atari 8-bit SIO peripherals (disk drives,
printer, cassette). It runs entirely on your device and communicates only with
Atari hardware you connect.

## Data we collect

**None.** AspeQt does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

- The app has **no internet access** and contains no analytics, advertising, or
  third-party tracking SDKs.
- There are no user accounts and no registration.

## Permissions and how they are used

- **USB host** — to talk to a SIO2PC-USB cable connected to your Atari.
- **Bluetooth** — to talk to a SIO2BT adapter (e.g. HC-06). Used only for the
  local serial link to your Atari; no data is sent anywhere else.
- **File access (Storage Access Framework)** — disk images, executables and
  folders are opened **only** when you explicitly pick them. Their contents are
  read and written **in place on your device**; nothing is uploaded or copied to
  any server.

## Children's privacy

AspeQt is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
