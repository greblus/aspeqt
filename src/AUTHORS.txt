R: device / 850 emulation
-------------------------

The R: device (850 interface emulation with a Hayes-compatible modem over
TCP) is derived from AspeQt-2k26 by Paul Jones <pjones1063@gmail.com>:

    https://github.com/pjones1063/AspeQt-2k26

Files taken from that project and adapted here:

    rdevice.cpp, rdevice.h      -- SIO-side 850 emulation, AT command set,
                                   Telnet negotiation
    phonedirectory.cpp/.h       -- BBS phonebook
    bbsdata.h                   -- phonebook entry struct
